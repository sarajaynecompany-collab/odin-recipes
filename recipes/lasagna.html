Lasagna

Description

A delicious lasagna recipe that is perfect for family dinners and gatherings. This recipe combines layers of rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and tender pasta sheets, all topped with melted cheese for a comforting and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet with the ground beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with half of the shredded mozzarella and all of the grated parmesan cheese.
  6. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Layer with noodles, ricotta mixture, and more meat sauce. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of meat sauce on top.
  7. Sprinkle the remaining shredded mozzarella cheese on top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let the lasagna cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
