Carbonara

Description

A classic Italian pasta dish that is simple yet incredibly flavorful. This carbonara recipe combines crispy pancetta, creamy eggs, and sharp Parmesan cheese to create a rich and satisfying meal that can be prepared in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 4 oz pancetta, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, cook the pancetta over medium heat until crispy. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Combine the cooked spaghetti with the pancetta and garlic mixture.
  5. Remove from heat and quickly stir in the egg and cheese mixture, ensuring it doesn't scramble.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy!
